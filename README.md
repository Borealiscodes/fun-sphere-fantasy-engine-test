# 📘 Overview

Fun‑Sphere‑Fantasy‑Engine‑Test is the home of the Tile Explainers, the Unified Math Spine, and the altitude‑layered narrative physics that define the FSFE universe.

This repo is not the production engine.  
It is the pedagogical and conceptual core of the Fun‑Sphere project —  
the place where:

- geometric AI becomes story physics,  
- math becomes play,  
- altitudes become narrative layers,  
- and complex operator theory becomes accessible through Tiles.

It is where the Unified Math Spine is taught, explored, refined, and made beautiful.

🎨 Purpose: Pedagogy, Art, and Meaning‑Making

This repo exists because:

- math can be taught through narrative,  
- geometric AI can be understood through play,  
- altitudes can be explained through characters,  
- holonomy can be made intuitive,  
- operator chains can be made visual,  
- and beauty can be built even under severe constraints.

The Tile Explainers are not jokes.  
They are pedagogical artifacts designed to make:

- reversible operators,  
- collapse surfaces,  
- manifold geometry,  
- spectral ecology,  
- and runtime physics  

accessible to people who would never read a formal preprint.

This repo is where math becomes meaning‑making.
