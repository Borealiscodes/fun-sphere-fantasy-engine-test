> Reversible operator chains acting on the world‑state manifold.

Let’s go.

---

🧮 Step 1: Quests Are Operators

A quest is:

\[
Q = (O1, O2, \ldots, O_n)
\]

Each \(O_i\) is an operator that:

- checks conditions  
- updates world‑state  
- modifies player state  
- interacts with events  
- interacts with zones  
- interacts with rasa  

Quests are math pipelines.

---

🔁 Step 2: Quests Are Composed

Quest progression is:

\[
Q' = Q \circ O{\text{combat}} \circ O{\text{inv}} \circ O_{E}
\]

This is the Tile way of saying:

> “Your quest updates by stacking math functions like LEGO bricks.”

Every time you:

- fight  
- pick up an item  
- trigger an event  

the quest algebra updates.

---

🧱 Step 3: Quests Have Preconditions

Each operator has a predicate:

\[
\Theta_i(W)
\]

If the condition is true:

\[
Oi(W) = W + \Delta Wi
\]

If false:

\[
O_i(W) = W
\]

This is the Tile way of saying:

> “You can’t finish the quest until you actually do the thing.”

---

🔁 Step 4: Quests Are Reversible

Because FSFE physics is reversible:

\[
Oi^{-1}(W) = W - \Delta Wi
\]

This means:

- undoing quest steps is possible  
- quest corruption is impossible  
- narrative paradoxes cannot occur  
- branching paths remain stable  

Your story is undo‑safe.

---

🌀 Step 5: Quests Interact With Everything

Quests talk to:

⚔️ Combat
(combat operators update quest state)

🎒 Inventory
(item pickups advance quests)

🗺️ Zones
(zone geometry affects quest conditions)

🐉 Monsters
(monster families are quest triggers)

(events modify quest progression)

🌀 Rasa
(emotional physics can influence quest outcomes)

Quests are networked narrative physics.

---

🧩 Step 6: Quests Are Governed

Governance ensures:

\[
G(Q) = Q \quad \text{iff stable}
\]

Otherwise:

\[
G(Q) = \Pi(Q)
\]

This prevents:

- broken quest chains  
- infinite loops  
- impossible states  
- paradoxical branches  
- narrative singularities  

Governance is the story safety system.
